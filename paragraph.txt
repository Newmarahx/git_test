A definition paragraph is a piece of writing that explains the full meaning, purpose, and real-world application of a specific word, term, or concept.
It typically follows a clear three-part structure:
• Topic Sentence: Names the term and gives its basic or dictionary definition.
• Supporting Sentences: Explains the idea further using examples, facts, context, or comparisons.
• Concluding Sentence: Summarizes the core point or wraps up the meaning.
